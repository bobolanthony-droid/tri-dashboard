Fitness and fatigue

Fitness (CTL) – your 42-day average daily training load. It rises slowly as you build; this is the number to grow toward race day.
Fatigue (ATL) – your 7-day average daily load. It reacts fast to hard weeks and drops fast with rest.
Form (TSB) = fitness − fatigue. Negative means you are carrying fatigue from training (normal in build weeks; below −30 is a lot). Around 0 to +15 on race day means fresh.
Ramp flag – a week where fitness rises by 5 or more. That is a steep build; sustained, it raises injury and illness risk.
